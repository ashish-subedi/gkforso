# PSC GK Practice (gkforso)

A free practice site for Lok Sewa Aayog (PSC) general knowledge: Nepal and world history, geography, the constitution, economy, current affairs, English, and aptitude & reasoning.

**Open it:** https://ashish-subedi.github.io/gkforso/

- Quizzes by category or topic, with an optional timer and negative marking.
- Spaced revision of the questions you got wrong or haven't seen for a while.
- Mistake book, category ratings and progress charts.
- Works on phones and computers, and keeps working offline once loaded.

## Your progress

Your answers and revision dates are saved in your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server.

- To keep a backup, or to move to another phone or computer, open **Progress & settings** and choose **Download progress file**.
- On the other device, choose **Load progress file**. Loading merges with what is already there.
- Clearing browser data or using a private window starts empty, so download your file now and then.

New questions are added to the same link over time, and your progress carries over.

## About the questions

The questions are collected from published PSC-style question sets and practice papers. Questions whose text was read by OCR show a picture of the printed original. If you find a wrong answer, use **Report a problem** on the question.
